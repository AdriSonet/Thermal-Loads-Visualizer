How a coil, a fan coil and an air handling unit take the load out of the air

The 3D room ends where the load reaches the system. This page follows it from there: water in a copper tube, fins, the air between them, and the water that condenses when the surface is cold enough. Then the unit that brings in outdoor air and recovers heat and moisture from the air it throws away.

Scroll to move through it. Every figure is calculated as you read; move a control and the drawing, the psychrometric chart and the balance change together.

Starting conditions are the simulated design day of the Zaragoza boarding lounge. Everything else is calculated on this page with a simplified model: it explains how the equipment behaves, it does not select a product.

The coil is marched row by row with a water film, the copper wall, fin efficiency and an air film; where the surface is below the local dew point it condenses (Lewis number 1). Recovery units use fixed efficiencies. Comfort is ISO 7730. The models and their tests are in the systems folder of the repository.
